# Copy system

Read this reference when naming the object, setting the price, or writing material, care, and object-note copy.

## Voice

Use neutral catalog language. State absurd things without winking at the reader. Avoid jokes, exclamation marks, fashion-house names, invented provenance, and generic art-school fog.

## Inanimate merchandise only

Every commerce field must refer exclusively to the chosen inanimate object. People and animals may appear in the image as supporting models, but they receive no product name, category, price, color, material, care instructions, scarcity language, ownership language, or CTA.

- Never derive a price from a caption or joke that assigns money to a living subject. Treat terms such as `彩礼 / bride price`, `身价 / human valuation`, `工资 / salary`, `出场费 / appearance fee`, `赏金 / bounty`, `赎金 / ransom`, `领养费 / adoption fee`, `拍卖价 / auction price`, and `出售 / for sale` as warning signals when a nearby amount points to a person or animal. Do not reuse that amount as the product price.
- Do not describe skin, hair, body shape, age, ethnicity, sex, disability, breed, fur, or other living traits as product color, material, condition, or specifications.
- Product names and manifesto lines must remain intelligible when the model is removed from the image. `MODEL SHOWN FOR SCALE` may be used only when clarification is genuinely needed.
- If the product copy could reasonably refer to the living subject rather than the object, do not write around the ambiguity. Return to object selection.

## Bilingual pairing

Write from one shared semantic brief, then produce English and Simplified Chinese as two native pieces of copy. They are siblings, not a source text and its translation.

- Keep the same visible object, product concept, price and currency, colors, materials, care meaning, and disclosure in both variants.
- Preserve the central conceptual beat while allowing different sentence structures and line breaks.
- Do not preserve English word order in Chinese. Do not add claims to make one language sound more impressive.
- Keep Chinese concrete and compressed. Prefer ordinary product nouns over translated fashion jargon.
- If a joke only works as an English pun, replace it with a Chinese line built from the same physical fact.
- Draft both variants before layout. Compare them as a fact pair, then proofread each independently.

## Required fields

### Product name

- Two to four functional words.
- Uppercase in the poster.
- Name what the object visibly is: `PAPER-SLEEVED DATA CABLE`, `CABLE TIE VASE`, `DELIVERY CUP HOLDER`.
- Prefer blunt nouns over poetic titles.

For Chinese, use a short functional compound such as `纸套数据线`, `扎带花瓶`, or `外卖杯架`. Do not pad the name with `系列`, `概念款`, or ornamental adjectives unless the object visibly requires them.

### Category

One familiar retail category such as `TABLE LAMP`, `HOME OBJECT`, `DESK ACCESSORY`, or `SCULPTURAL STORAGE`. Pair it with an ordinary Chinese retail category such as `台灯`, `家居物件`, `桌面配件`, or `雕塑式收纳`.

### Price

The price should be shocking but formatted like a plausible luxury listing.

- Anchor it loosely to the object’s ordinary value, then multiply by roughly 25–200.
- Prefer legible catalog prices such as `$1,250`, `$2,490`, or `$4,800` over comedic random numbers.
- Do not claim a sale, limited stock count, or real market value.
- Keep the exact formatted price and currency identical across the paired posters unless the user explicitly requests localized currencies and supplies or approves the conversion.
- Anchor the absurd multiplier to the ordinary object's plausible value, never to a person, animal, relationship, labor, body, or identity.

### Color

Describe observed colors with dry compound names: `OFF WHITE / INDUSTRIAL GREY`, `TRANSLUCENT BLUE / DIRTY SILVER`. Chinese equivalents should sound like terse swatch labels: `灰白 / 工业灰`, `半透明蓝 / 暗银`.

### Material

Translate real materials into accurate technical nouns without upgrading them dishonestly.

- `plastic bag` → `TRANSLUCENT POLYMER`
- `zip ties` → `NYLON FASTENERS`
- `cardboard` → `PRESSED CELLULOSE BOARD`
- `cheap steel tube` → `POWDER-COATED STEEL`

Never add `Made in Italy`, proprietary technology, certification, or a material that is not visible or supplied by the user.

Translate the material facts, not the marketing register. `RIGID TRANSLUCENT POLYMER / BRUSHED ALUMINUM` can become `硬质半透明聚合物 / 拉丝铝`, without adding `航空级`, `匠造`, or other unsupported upgrades.

### Care

Use two or three short instructions. One should expose the object’s lost original function.

- `DO NOT FOLD`
- `DO NOT USE FOR CARRYING OBJECTS`
- `CLEAN WITH A SOFT DRY CLOTH`
- `KEEP AWAY FROM HIGH TEMPERATURES`

Chinese care lines should stay directive and short: `请勿折叠`, `请勿用于装载物品`, `使用柔软干布清洁`.

### Object note

Write two compact sentences grounded in visible facts:

1. Describe the ordinary form made permanent or reassigned.
2. Describe what function disappears and what formal trace remains.

Good:

> A disposable silhouette reconstructed as a permanent object. The function disappears. The shape remains.

Avoid vague language such as “a meditation on modernity,” “where memory meets form,” or claims about consumer culture that the image does not support.

A paired Chinese note may be structurally different while preserving the same observation:

> 一次性轮廓被重新制造为永久物件。功能消失，形状留下。

## Fixed project copy

The project mark, AI-parody safety disclosure, and non-affiliation disclosure belong to the series, not the individual product. Do not generate them, paraphrase them, or include them in `variants`; the renderer inserts the locale-appropriate set automatically. The safety disclosure must remain direct and unambiguous so a finished image cannot reasonably be presented as a real listing.

- English project mark: `DON'T LET THEIR DESIGNER KNOW`
- Chinese project mark: `别让他们的设计师知道`
- English safety disclosure: `AI-GENERATED PARODY · NOT A REAL PRODUCT · FOR ENTERTAINMENT ONLY`
- Chinese safety disclosure: `AI 生成戏仿 · 非真实商品 · 仅供娱乐`
- English disclosure: `Independent speculative design. Not affiliated with any fashion house.`
- Chinese disclosure: `独立概念设计，与任何时装品牌无关。`
